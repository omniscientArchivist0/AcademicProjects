# Ethics
## Description
Ethics is a small intermission that the university provides to inspire the students to analyse the world around them and be able to develop critical thinking and empathy. Finishting the course with a huge writing project involved a lot of soft skills, some that I had to develop substantially.
## Hard Skills
- Research: Proficiency in finding scientifical, precise answers to proposed questions.
- Presentation: Making complex/elaborate ideas into simple-to-explain, visual-aligned documents.
## Soft Skills
- Adaptability: Being able to learn different skills even in situations where you aren't comfortable with them.
- Communication: Relaying your perspective to other people in a calm, controlled and effective way, either to convince or learn with them.
- Empathy: The ability to relate to other people and understand their point of view.
