## Description
Those projects were made for Software Engineering 2 and Computer Graphics classes. Both of them relate directly to programming, but to a more deep side of it. Probably two of my favorite projects I worked on.
## Hard Skills
- **Design**: Attention to details, how they affect the world around them and how they connect to each other both visually and logically.
- **Presentation**: Making complex/elaborate ideas into simple-to-explain, visual-aligned documents.
## Soft Skills
- **Adaptability**: Being able to learn different skills even in situations where you aren't comfortable with them.
- **Proactivity**: Working with so many people leads to the need of being able to relay your ideas and progress in clean terms, receiving and giving help when needed.
